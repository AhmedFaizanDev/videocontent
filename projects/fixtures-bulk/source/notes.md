Cardiac arrest: the heart stops beating effectively; an electrical problem. In ventricular fibrillation the ventricles quiver and eject no blood. Chest compressions push blood out of the heart. Compression rate 100 to 120 a minute. Survival to discharge: 10.5% out of hospital, 23.6% in hospital for adults. The Chain of Survival: recognition, compressions, defibrillation, advanced care.
