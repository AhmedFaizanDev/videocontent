## Short Description
Patient identification, phlebotomy, labelling, transport and rejection criteria — where most lab errors begin.

## Full Description
The pre-examination phase is where most laboratory errors begin. This course covers test requisition, positive patient identification, phlebotomy technique, order of draw, container selection, labelling, sample transport, temperature control and documented rejection criteria. ISO 15189:2022 and NABL require laboratories to control these steps and record rejections, making this training essential for collection and transport staff.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply two-identifier patient identification before every sample collection episode
2. Demonstrate correct phlebotomy technique, order of draw and container selection
3. Explain transport, storage and temperature requirements for common laboratory specimens
4. Use written rejection criteria to accept or reject received samples consistently

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can collect and label samples without identification or container errors
2. Learners can package and transport specimens within stated time and temperature limits
3. Learners can document sample rejection with reason and communicate it to requesting units
4. Learners can recognise haemolysis, clotting and dilution problems arising from collection practice

## Who Can Take This Course
Phlebotomists, laboratory technicians and technologists, collection centre staff, staff nurses who draw samples, ward attendants handling transport, sample reception clerks, and MLT or nursing students on posting.
