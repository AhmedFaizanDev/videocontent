## Short Description
Structured handover and documentation — a frequent audit finding, fixed.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme teaches structured clinical handover using SBAR along with disciplined nursing documentation. It covers shift handover, transfer and discharge handover, telephone orders, read-back verification, entry timing, corrections and abbreviation control. Incomplete handover and documentation are recurring findings during NABH 6th Edition assessment and in medico-legal review, and structured formats measurably reduce information loss between shifts and departments.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain how communication failure during handover contributes to patient harm and audit findings
2. Apply the SBAR format to shift, transfer, telephone and escalation communications
3. Demonstrate read-back verification for verbal and telephonic orders and critical results
4. Use documentation rules on timing, signatures, corrections and approved abbreviations

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can give a complete SBAR handover for a complex patient within a set time
2. Learners can record a telephone order with read-back and countersignature as policy requires
3. Learners can write nursing notes that are legible, timed, signed and free of unapproved abbreviations
4. Learners can audit a handover against a checklist and identify missing information

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges and nursing supervisors, ICU and emergency nurses, Medical Officers and resident doctors, physiotherapists, medical records staff, and nursing students preparing for clinical responsibility.
