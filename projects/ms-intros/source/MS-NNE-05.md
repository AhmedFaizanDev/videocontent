## Short Description
Ready your nursing service for the fast-growing Nursing Excellence certification.

## Full Description
This programme prepares a hospital nursing service for NABH Nursing Excellence certification. It covers nursing standards, staffing and skill mix, competency assessment, nursing documentation, care planning, patient safety indicators and nursing quality audits. Hospitals seeking the certification need their nursing leadership and bedside teams to work to a common standard, with records and indicator data that demonstrate consistent practice across all shifts and wards.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the scope and requirements of NABH Nursing Excellence certification
2. Apply competency assessment and credentialling methods to the nursing workforce
3. Use nursing quality indicators to monitor and improve bedside care
4. Plan nursing documentation and care plans that meet certification requirements

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can prepare ward level evidence required for a certification assessment
2. Learners can collect, analyse and present nursing quality indicator data monthly
3. Learners can carry out competency assessments and maintain individual nursing records
4. Learners can identify practice gaps in nursing care and plan corrective action

## Who Can Take This Course
Nursing superintendents, deputy nursing superintendents, ward in-charges, staff nurses, nurse educators, quality coordinators and final year nursing students or interns preparing for hospital nursing roles.
