## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Frontline conflict management and safe de-escalation.

## Full Description
This three-hour refresher helps frontline hospital staff manage angry, anxious or aggressive patients and attendants. It covers early warning signs, listening and acknowledgement, tone and body language, setting limits, managing waiting-time and billing disputes, breaking bad news calmly, personal safety positioning, when to call security, and incident reporting after a violent or threatening encounter.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify early behavioural signs that a patient or attendant may become aggressive
2. Apply listening, acknowledgement and calm language to reduce rising tension
3. Demonstrate safe positioning, exit awareness and escalation to security personnel
4. Plan post-incident reporting, debriefing and staff support after a violent encounter

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can defuse a common complaint before it becomes a confrontation
2. Learners can maintain a safe distance and exit route during aggressive encounters
3. Learners can call for security help at the right point
4. Learners can record an incident accurately for hospital review and action

## Who Can Take This Course
Front office and registration staff, security personnel, staff nurses, ward in-charges, billing and insurance desk staff, Medical Officers, counsellors, housekeeping supervisors, ambulance crew and hospital administration interns.
