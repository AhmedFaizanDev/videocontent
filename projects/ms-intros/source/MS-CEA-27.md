# Clinical Establishments Act & Statutory Licensing (MS-CEA-27)

## Course Code
MS-CEA-27

## Course Name
Clinical Establishments Act & Statutory Licensing

## Category
Medico-Legal & Regulatory Compliance

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Keep registrations, licences and statutory displays in order across the hospital.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers registration and licensing obligations under the Clinical Establishments Act and state adaptations, along with linked statutory requirements such as biomedical waste authorisation under BMW Rules 2016, AERB licences for radiology, PC-PNDT registration, pharmacy and blood bank licences, and mandatory public displays. Participants learn to build a licence register with renewal tracking so that no statutory approval lapses unnoticed.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the registration, standards and record keeping duties under the Clinical Establishments Act
2. Identify every statutory licence, authorisation and registration applicable to the establishment
3. Plan a licence register with ownership, validity dates and advance renewal reminders
4. Apply rules on mandatory displays, rate lists and record retention required by regulators

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can compile a complete statutory licence register for their hospital or centre
2. Learners can track renewal timelines and initiate applications before expiry dates
3. Learners can verify that mandatory boards, rate displays and notices are correctly posted
4. Learners can assemble documents required during an inspection by a statutory authority

## Who Can Take This Course
Hospital administrators and managers, medical superintendents, quality managers, compliance and legal officers, HR and facility managers, purchase and stores heads, and hospital administration students or management trainees.
