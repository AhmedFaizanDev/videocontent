## Short Description
Culture, ethics and professional conduct as a standard onboarding module.

## Full Description
A short onboarding module on professional conduct in hospital settings. It covers the hospital code of conduct, patient confidentiality and the obligations introduced by the DPDP Act 2023, informed consent, conflict of interest, gifts and referrals, respectful behaviour towards patients and colleagues, social media use, and the workplace expectations set by the POSH Act 2013 for a safe and dignified environment.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the hospital code of conduct and the behaviour expected from staff
2. Apply confidentiality rules to patient records, conversations and digital information
3. Identify conflicts of interest, improper referrals and gift-related risks
4. Explain employee rights and duties under the POSH Act 2013

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can protect patient information in wards, corridors and on messaging applications
2. Learners can recognise unprofessional conduct and use the correct reporting channel
3. Learners can describe consent requirements before procedures, photography and data sharing
4. Learners can act respectfully towards patients, attendants and colleagues in daily work

## Who Can Take This Course
All new joiners across clinical and non-clinical departments, including doctors, nurses, technicians, administrative staff, housekeeping, security and outsourced personnel, along with students, interns and trainees during induction.
