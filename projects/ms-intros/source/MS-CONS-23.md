## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Take, document and defend valid consent — high-risk, high-litigation and often done poorly.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers the legal basis of informed consent in India, the elements of valid consent, capacity and substitute decision making, consent for minors, emergencies and high-risk procedures, and defensible documentation. NABH 6th Edition specifies a defined list of procedures needing written consent, and PC-PNDT and MTP requirements carry their own consent formats. Poor consent records are a frequent finding in litigation.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the legal elements that make consent valid under Indian law and professional regulation
2. Identify situations requiring written consent, substitute consent or emergency treatment without consent
3. Apply consent procedures for minors, patients lacking capacity and language barriers
4. Demonstrate documentation of the consent discussion, risks disclosed and withdrawal of consent

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can decide who may lawfully give consent for a given patient and procedure
2. Learners can conduct a consent discussion covering risks, alternatives and expected outcomes
3. Learners can complete a consent form that withstands audit and medico-legal scrutiny
4. Learners can identify defective consent records during a medical record review

## Who Can Take This Course
Consultants, resident doctors, Medical Officers, staff nurses, OT and procedure room nurses, medical records officers, quality and medico-legal staff, and medical or nursing interns. Required for all staff taking or witnessing consent.
