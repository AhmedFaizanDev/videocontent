## Full Description
This practical course teaches correct lead placement, systematic rhythm strip reading, recognition of sinus rhythms, atrial fibrillation, heart blocks, ventricular tachycardia, asystole and ischaemic changes, along with artefact identification. Nurses in emergency departments, intensive care units, wards and primary health centres often record the first tracing, so timely recognition and escalation to the treating doctor shortens the door to treatment interval.

## Course Objectives
1. Demonstrate correct electrode placement and acquisition of a twelve-lead ECG
2. Apply a systematic approach to rate, rhythm, axis and interval assessment
3. Identify life-threatening arrhythmias and acute ischaemic changes on a tracing
4. Use structured escalation to communicate abnormal findings to the treating doctor

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can record a technically adequate ECG free of common artefacts
2. Learners can recognise atrial fibrillation, heart block and ventricular tachycardia reliably
3. Learners can identify ST segment changes needing immediate medical review
4. Learners can escalate an abnormal tracing using a structured handover format

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, emergency and ICU nurses, ward in-charges, cardiac care technicians, ANMs, community health officers, Medical Officers at primary health centres, and nursing students or interns.
