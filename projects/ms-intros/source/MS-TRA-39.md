# Basic Trauma Management (MS-TRA-39)

## Course Code
MS-TRA-39

## Course Name
Basic Trauma Management

## Category
Emergency & Critical Care

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Initial trauma assessment and stabilisation for ED and surgical teams.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers initial trauma assessment and stabilisation using a primary and secondary survey approach. It includes airway with cervical spine protection, breathing and chest injury recognition, circulation and haemorrhage control, disability assessment, exposure, log roll, splinting and safe transfer. Indian emergency departments and trauma-receiving centres need consistent first-hour care, and the content follows WHO trauma care guidance.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply the primary survey sequence to identify and treat immediately life threatening injuries
2. Demonstrate airway management with manual cervical spine stabilisation and collar application
3. Use direct pressure, tourniquet and pelvic binder techniques to control major external haemorrhage
4. Plan safe packaging, monitoring and interhospital transfer of the stabilised trauma patient

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can complete a structured primary survey on a simulated multiply injured patient
2. Learners can recognise tension pneumothorax, haemorrhagic shock and head injury deterioration promptly
3. Learners can perform a log roll and apply splints with adequate immobilisation
4. Learners can hand over a trauma patient using a structured format to the receiving team

## Who Can Take This Course
Emergency department nurses and Medical Officers, casualty medical officers, surgical and orthopaedic residents, ICU nurses, ambulance paramedics and EMTs, OT nurses, and final year medical or nursing students.
