# Communication Skills & Breaking Bad News (MS-COMM-52)

## Course Code
MS-COMM-52

## Course Name
Communication Skills & Breaking Bad News

## Category
Patient Experience & Soft Skills

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Clear, compassionate communication for doctors and nurses.

## Full Description
This short course covers structured clinical communication, active listening, explaining diagnosis and treatment in plain language, managing anxious or angry attendants, and a stepwise approach to breaking bad news and discussing death. Clear communication reduces complaints and disputes in busy Indian outpatient departments and wards, and supports the patient rights and education requirements of the NABH 6th Edition standard.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the elements of effective clinician and patient communication
2. Apply a stepwise protocol for delivering bad news to families
3. Demonstrate active listening and empathy during difficult conversations
4. Use plain language to explain diagnosis, treatment options and costs

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can conduct a bad news conversation using a structured sequence
2. Learners can de-escalate an angry attendant without losing clinical control
3. Learners can check patient understanding before concluding a consultation
4. Learners can document sensitive discussions and consent conversations accurately

## Who Can Take This Course
Consultants, Medical Officers, postgraduate residents, staff nurses, ward in-charges, counsellors, front office and patient relations staff, medical social workers, and medical or nursing interns.
