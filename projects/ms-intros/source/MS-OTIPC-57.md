## Short Description
Theatre asepsis, traffic control, environmental cleaning and sterile technique for OT teams.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers operating theatre asepsis, zoning and traffic control, surgical scrub and gowning, environmental cleaning schedules, sterile field maintenance, instrument handling and theatre ventilation monitoring. NABH 6th Edition infection control and surgical care standards require documented theatre practices and surveillance of surgical site infection. Content is aligned to WHO surgical safety and infection prevention guidance and Indian theatre working conditions.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain theatre zoning, air handling and traffic control principles that limit microbial contamination
2. Demonstrate surgical hand antisepsis, gowning, gloving and sterile field maintenance without breach
3. Apply between-case, end-of-day and terminal cleaning protocols with correct disinfectants and contact times
4. Plan surgical site infection surveillance and theatre environmental monitoring for the operating complex

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can identify and correct a break in sterile technique during a procedure
2. Learners can carry out surgical scrub, gowning and gloving to the required standard
3. Learners can schedule and document theatre cleaning with appropriate disinfectant and contact time
4. Learners can collect surgical site infection data and report it to the infection control committee

## Who Can Take This Course
OT nurses and scrub nurses, OT technicians, anaesthesia technicians, surgeons and anaesthetists, CSSD staff, OT housekeeping and attendants, infection control nurses, and nursing or OT technology students posted to theatre.
