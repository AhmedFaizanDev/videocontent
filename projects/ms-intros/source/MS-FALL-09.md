## Learning Hours

## Short Description
Assess risk and prevent falls — a common, monitored incident on every ward.

## Full Description
This three-hour refresher covers fall risk assessment, environmental and patient level preventive measures, post-fall management and incident reporting. Falls are a monitored patient safety indicator under NABH 6th Edition, and every inpatient area is expected to screen patients on admission and after any change in condition. The course uses common ward scenarios including elderly, sedated and post-operative patients.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain intrinsic and extrinsic factors that increase inpatient fall risk in Indian wards
2. Apply a validated fall risk assessment scale on admission, transfer and condition change
3. Identify environmental hazards and design preventive measures for high-risk patients
4. Demonstrate correct post-fall assessment, documentation and incident reporting steps

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can score a patient's fall risk correctly and record it in the nursing notes
2. Learners can select and implement preventive measures matched to the assessed risk level
3. Learners can brief a patient and attendant on fall precautions in plain language
4. Learners can complete a post-fall incident report with timing, injury assessment and actions taken

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, nursing assistants, physiotherapists, Medical Officers, patient care attendants, housekeeping supervisors, and nursing students on clinical postings in wards, ICUs and day care areas.
