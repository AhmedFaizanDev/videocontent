## Short Description
The flagship annual course for all staff — standard precautions, HAIs and outbreak response.

## Full Description
The hospital's flagship annual infection control programme. It covers standard and transmission-based precautions, hand hygiene, personal protective equipment, injection and sharps safety, environmental cleaning, device-associated infection bundles, antimicrobial stewardship basics and outbreak response. NABH 6th Edition requires a functioning infection control programme with trained staff, and BMW Rules 2016 obligations for segregation and handling are addressed alongside.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain standard precautions and transmission-based precautions for common hospital pathogens
2. Demonstrate WHO five moments of hand hygiene and correct PPE sequence
3. Apply care bundles that reduce catheter, ventilator and bloodstream infections
4. Identify notifiable events and the hospital pathway for outbreak reporting

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can perform hand hygiene and PPE donning and doffing correctly
2. Learners can isolate a patient using the appropriate precaution category and signage
3. Learners can segregate biomedical waste into the correct colour-coded category
4. Learners can escalate a suspected cluster of infections to the infection control team

## Who Can Take This Course
All hospital staff including doctors, staff nurses, technicians, housekeeping and laundry workers, CSSD staff, dietary and security personnel, administrators, and students or interns; required annually for clinical and non-clinical staff.
