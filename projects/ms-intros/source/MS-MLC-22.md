## Short Description
Handle MLCs, police requisitions and evidence correctly — a frequent source of hospital liability.

## Full Description
Medico-legal cases are a frequent source of hospital liability. This course covers identifying a case as medico-legal, MLC register entries, intimation to police, injury description and documentation, custody of clothing and specimens, chain of custody, dying declarations, handling police requisitions and summons, and release of records. It supports obligations under the Clinical Establishments Act and hospital record-keeping standards.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify which presentations must be registered as medico-legal cases
2. Apply correct MLC register entry, intimation and documentation procedures
3. Demonstrate chain of custody for clothing, samples and other evidence
4. Explain lawful responses to police requisitions, summons and record requests

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can register an MLC completely and inform police within required timelines
2. Learners can describe injuries factually without recording opinions beyond their competence
3. Learners can preserve and hand over evidence with a signed custody record
4. Learners can respond to a police requisition following hospital and legal procedure

## Who Can Take This Course
Emergency Medical Officers, casualty staff nurses, resident doctors, medical records officers, front office and admission staff, duty managers, security supervisors, hospital administrators, and interns posted in casualty; required for all emergency staff.
