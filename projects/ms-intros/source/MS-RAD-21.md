# Radiation Safety (AERB) for Imaging Staff (MS-RAD-21)

## Course Code
MS-RAD-21

## Course Name
Radiation Safety (AERB) for Imaging Staff

## Category
Waste, Fire & Facility Safety

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Mandatory safety training for radiology, cath-lab and radiotherapy teams.

## Full Description
This six-hour course covers radiation protection for imaging and therapy staff under AERB requirements. It explains ionising radiation and dose units, time, distance and shielding, personnel monitoring with TLD badges, dose limits, quality assurance of equipment, protection of pregnant patients and staff, layout and warning signage, and the licensing and reporting duties of radiation facilities in India.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain radiation quantities, dose limits and biological effects relevant to imaging staff
2. Apply time, distance and shielding principles during radiography, fluoroscopy and cath-lab procedures
3. Use TLD badges and dose records to monitor occupational exposure correctly
4. Identify AERB licensing, layout approval and incident reporting obligations for radiation facilities

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can position themselves and use shielding to minimise occupational dose
2. Learners can explain personnel monitoring results and act on raised readings
3. Learners can screen patients for pregnancy before an imaging examination
4. Learners can list the records an AERB inspection expects from the department

## Who Can Take This Course
Radiographers, radiology technologists, radiologists, cath-lab nurses and technicians, radiotherapy and nuclear medicine staff, RSOs, biomedical engineers and radiography interns; required for all staff working with radiation sources.
