# Hospital Disaster Management & Mass Casualty (MS-DIS-40)

## Course Code
MS-DIS-40

## Course Name
Hospital Disaster Management & Mass Casualty

## Category
Emergency & Critical Care

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
12

## Short Description
Disaster preparedness and drills for a coordinated hospital response.

## Full Description
This certification-grade programme covers hospital hazard vulnerability analysis, the incident command system, code activation, triage in mass casualty events, surge capacity, evacuation, communication lines, supply and morgue planning, and post-event review. NABH 6th Edition requires a documented disaster plan with periodic mock drills, and hospitals in India must be ready for road traffic incidents, fires, floods and disease outbreaks.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the components of a hospital disaster plan and incident command structure
2. Apply mass casualty triage methods to sort patients by clinical priority
3. Plan surge capacity, staffing call-up and supply arrangements for a major incident
4. Demonstrate evacuation and communication procedures during a simulated emergency

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can state their assigned role and reporting line during code activation
2. Learners can triage simulated casualties correctly under time pressure
3. Learners can conduct a mock drill and record the debrief findings
4. Learners can identify gaps in the hospital disaster plan after review

## Who Can Take This Course
Hospital administrators, emergency physicians, Medical Officers, nursing superintendents, ward in-charges, staff nurses, security and fire safety officers, housekeeping and transport supervisors; required for all staff with disaster roles.
