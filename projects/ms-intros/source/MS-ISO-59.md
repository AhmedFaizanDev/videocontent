## Short Description
The standard NABL accredits against — requirements, QMS structure and every staff member’s role in it.

## Full Description
A detailed introduction to ISO 15189:2022, the standard NABL accredits medical laboratories against. The programme explains the structural, resource, process and management system requirements, document and record control, competence, and each staff member's role in maintaining the quality management system. Laboratories seeking or holding NABL accreditation need every technologist and officer to understand these obligations.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the structure and clause layout of ISO 15189:2022
2. Identify resource, process and management system requirements applicable to each laboratory role
3. Apply document control and record retention rules within the laboratory quality system
4. Plan evidence of competence, training and impartiality required during NABL assessment

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can locate the clause governing any routine laboratory activity
2. Learners can maintain controlled documents, forms and records to accreditation requirements
3. Learners can describe their own responsibilities within the laboratory quality management system
4. Learners can prepare departmental evidence for an internal or NABL assessment

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory quality managers, technical managers, pathologists, microbiologists, biochemists, senior and junior laboratory technologists, phlebotomists, laboratory administrators, and MLT students or interns posted in accredited laboratories.
