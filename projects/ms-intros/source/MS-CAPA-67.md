## Short Description
Identify risks, manage nonconforming work and close corrective actions that stick.

## Full Description
This course covers risk-based thinking, control of nonconforming work and corrective action in medical laboratories. It explains risk identification and assessment, immediate containment, root cause analysis using structured tools, corrective action planning, effectiveness checks and closure records. ISO 15189:2022 requires laboratories to address risk and nonconformities, and NABL assessors routinely examine how well these actions are closed.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify risks to patient safety and result quality across laboratory processes
2. Apply a structured root cause analysis method to laboratory nonconformities
3. Plan corrective actions with owners, timelines and measurable effectiveness checks
4. Explain the difference between correction, corrective action and preventive improvement

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can complete a risk register entry with likelihood, impact and control
2. Learners can investigate a nonconformity and reach a defensible root cause
3. Learners can close a CAPA record with documented evidence of effectiveness
4. Learners can distinguish recurring issues needing systemic change from isolated one-off errors

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory quality managers, technical managers, section in-charges, senior technologists, pathologists and consultants, quality coordinators, and internal auditors responsible for nonconformity and CAPA records.
