# Emergency & Triage Nursing (MS-TRI-37)

## Course Code
MS-TRI-37

## Course Name
Emergency & Triage Nursing

## Category
Emergency & Critical Care

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
12

## Short Description
Rapid triage and prioritisation for casualty and emergency-department staff.

## Full Description
A twelve-hour course in emergency and triage nursing for casualty departments. It covers triage systems and colour coding, primary and secondary survey, airway and breathing support, shock recognition, trauma and polytrauma handling, stroke and chest pain pathways, poisoning, mass casualty and disaster response, and medico-legal documentation. Indian emergency departments face high, unpredictable loads and need staff who can sort and prioritise reliably.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply a structured triage system to sort patients arriving at casualty
2. Demonstrate primary and secondary survey on trauma and medical emergency patients
3. Identify early shock, airway compromise and time-critical conditions needing immediate escalation
4. Plan departmental response to mass casualty incidents and surge situations

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can assign a triage category within the first minutes of arrival
2. Learners can stabilise airway, breathing and circulation before definitive care begins
3. Learners can activate stroke, trauma and cardiac pathways without delay
4. Learners can maintain emergency and medico-legal records during a busy shift

## Who Can Take This Course
Emergency and casualty nurses, ward nurses rotating to emergency, Medical Officers, emergency physicians, paramedics, ambulance staff, triage assistants and nursing or emergency medicine interns posted to casualty.
