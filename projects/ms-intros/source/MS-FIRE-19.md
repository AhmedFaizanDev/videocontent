## Category

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Required for Fire NOC and NABH FMS — response, extinguishers and evacuation.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers fire prevention, detection and alarm systems, extinguisher selection and use, hospital evacuation of dependent patients, assembly points and mock drill conduct. Hospitals need this for Fire NOC renewal under state fire service rules and for NABH 6th Edition facility management and safety standards. Content includes fire load control in stores, oxygen manifolds, electrical panels and record keeping for drills.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain common fire hazards in hospital wards, stores, kitchens and oxygen handling areas
2. Identify fire classes and select the correct extinguisher type for each situation
3. Demonstrate extinguisher operation, alarm activation and the RACE response sequence
4. Plan horizontal and vertical evacuation of dependent patients with defined staff roles

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can locate alarm points, extinguishers, hydrants and exit routes on their own floor
2. Learners can operate a fire extinguisher correctly on a live or simulated fire
3. Learners can carry out their assigned role during an evacuation of bedbound patients
4. Learners can record a mock drill with timings, observations and corrective actions identified

## Who Can Take This Course
All hospital staff including nurses, doctors, technicians, housekeeping, security, engineering and maintenance, stores, kitchen and front office personnel, plus interns and students. Required annually for all clinical and non-clinical staff.
