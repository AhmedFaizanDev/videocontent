# Patient Rights & Responsibilities (MS-PR-49)

## Course Code
MS-PR-49

## Course Name
Patient Rights & Responsibilities

## Category
Onboarding, Ethics & HR Compliance

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
A dedicated NABH chapter, for every patient-facing member of staff.

## Full Description
A three-hour refresher on the patient rights and responsibilities chapter of NABH 6th Edition. It covers information and informed consent, privacy and dignity, confidentiality and the DPDP Act 2023, refusal of treatment, second opinion, tariff transparency and estimates, protection of vulnerable patients, complaint and grievance handling, and the responsibilities patients and attendants carry within the hospital.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the patient rights recognised under NABH 6th Edition and Indian law
2. Apply informed consent practice for procedures, anaesthesia, blood transfusion and research
3. Demonstrate protection of patient privacy, dignity and confidentiality during routine care
4. Use the hospital grievance procedure to record and resolve patient complaints

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can display and explain patient rights to patients and attendants
2. Learners can obtain valid informed consent and document it correctly
3. Learners can safeguard patient information in line with DPDP Act 2023 duties
4. Learners can log a complaint and follow it through to documented closure

## Who Can Take This Course
All patient-facing staff including nurses, Medical Officers, front office and billing staff, security, housekeeping, technicians, counsellors and interns; required annually for every clinical and non-clinical employee.
