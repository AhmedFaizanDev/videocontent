## Short Description
Newborn resuscitation and NICU essentials for obstetric and paediatric units.

## Full Description
This twelve-hour course covers newborn resuscitation and NICU care following WHO and national newborn care guidance. It includes antenatal risk assessment, delivery room preparation, the resuscitation algorithm, positive pressure ventilation, chest compressions, umbilical access, thermal care, kangaroo mother care, feeding, sepsis screening, jaundice management and safe transport. Labour rooms and newborn units across India need staff competent in the first golden minute.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply the neonatal resuscitation algorithm from initial steps to advanced support
2. Demonstrate effective positive pressure ventilation and chest compressions on newborn manikins
3. Plan delivery room preparation, equipment checks and team roles before every birth
4. Identify neonatal sepsis, hypothermia, hypoglycaemia and jaundice needing prompt intervention

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can complete initial newborn steps within the first minute of birth
2. Learners can ventilate a non-breathing newborn effectively with bag and mask
3. Learners can maintain thermal care and support kangaroo mother care in the unit
4. Learners can prepare and escort a sick newborn for safe referral transport

## Who Can Take This Course
Labour room and NICU nurses, staff nurses, ANMs, paediatricians, obstetricians, Medical Officers, neonatal technicians and nursing, paediatric or midwifery interns posted to maternity and newborn units.
