## Short Description
Build the in-house audit team NABH expects, with a repeatable internal audit method.

## Full Description
This certification-grade programme prepares hospital staff to plan, conduct and report internal audits against NABH 6th Edition standards. It covers audit scheduling, checklist design, objective evidence, interview technique, nonconformity grading and corrective action follow-up. Every accredited Indian hospital must run internal audits before assessment, and an in-house team makes that requirement affordable, repeatable and genuinely useful for improvement.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the purpose and scope of internal audit under NABH 6th Edition
2. Plan an annual audit programme covering all departments and standards
3. Apply checklist and sampling methods to gather objective audit evidence
4. Grade nonconformities and verify corrective actions through documented follow-up

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can prepare a departmental audit plan with scope, criteria and timelines
2. Learners can conduct an opening meeting, audit interview and closing meeting confidently
3. Learners can write clear nonconformity statements supported by objective evidence
4. Learners can track corrective actions to closure and report to quality committees

## Who Can Take This Course
Quality managers, quality coordinators, NABH coordinators, nursing superintendents, department heads, senior staff nurses, hospital administrators and postgraduate hospital administration students nominated to the internal audit team.
