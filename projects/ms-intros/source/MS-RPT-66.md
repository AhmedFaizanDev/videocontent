## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Controlled documents, records retention, report release and critical-value communication.

## Full Description
A six-hour course on laboratory documentation and result release. It covers controlled document numbering, version control, amendment and withdrawal, record retention periods, report content and format, amended reports, turnaround time monitoring and critical value communication with documented read-back. ISO 15189:2022 and NABL require traceable documents and timely critical result alerts, and this course gives laboratory staff the working practice behind those clauses.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain document control principles for laboratory manuals, SOPs, forms and formats
2. Apply retention rules to laboratory records, worksheets and archived reports
3. Demonstrate correct report release, verification and amendment procedures for patient results
4. Use a critical value list and read-back method to communicate urgent findings

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can issue, revise and withdraw a controlled document with full traceability
2. Learners can release and amend patient reports without losing the original record
3. Learners can escalate a critical value and document the communication correctly
4. Learners can monitor turnaround time and report delays to the quality manager

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory technicians, report despatch and front office staff, section in-charges, quality managers, pathologists, data entry operators and MLT interns; required for all staff involved in result release.
