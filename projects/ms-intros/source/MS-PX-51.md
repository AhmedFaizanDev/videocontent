# Patient Experience & Service Excellence (MS-PX-51)

## Course Code
MS-PX-51

## Course Name
Patient Experience & Service Excellence

## Category
Patient Experience & Soft Skills

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Lift satisfaction scores, reviews and repeat footfall through better patient experience.

## Full Description
This three-hour programme covers practical patient experience improvement: first contact behaviour, waiting time communication, empathy in difficult conversations, attendant management, complaint handling and service recovery, and feedback collection. NABH 6th Edition requires a patient feedback and grievance redressal system, and NQAS assesses patient satisfaction in public facilities. The course addresses realistic Indian outpatient, inpatient and emergency settings.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain how service touchpoints across the patient journey shape overall satisfaction ratings
2. Demonstrate empathetic communication with anxious patients and demanding attendants
3. Apply a service recovery approach to complaints, delays and billing disputes
4. Use feedback and grievance data to identify recurring service failures for correction

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can greet, orient and set expectations for a patient at first contact
2. Learners can de-escalate an upset attendant without arguing or escalating the situation
3. Learners can log a complaint and close it within the hospital's defined timeline
4. Learners can summarise feedback trends and propose one practical service improvement

## Who Can Take This Course
Front office and reception staff, billing and insurance desk personnel, staff nurses and ward in-charges, patient relations executives, security personnel, housekeeping supervisors, Medical Officers, and hospital administration interns.

Online, self-paced (mobile and desktop)
