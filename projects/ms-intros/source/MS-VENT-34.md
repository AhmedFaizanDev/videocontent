## Short Description
Ventilator and airway essentials for ICU, HDU and emergency nurses.

## Full Description
A practical programme on airway management and mechanical ventilation for nurses working in intensive care, high dependency and emergency areas. It covers airway assessment, intubation assistance, endotracheal tube and tracheostomy care, cuff pressure, suctioning, common ventilator modes and alarms, weaning support, and the ventilator-associated pneumonia prevention bundle recommended in WHO and national infection control guidance.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain common ventilator modes, settings and the alarms associated with each
2. Demonstrate safe suctioning, cuff pressure checking and tracheostomy care
3. Apply the ventilator-associated pneumonia prevention bundle during every nursing shift
4. Identify airway emergencies such as tube displacement, obstruction and accidental extubation

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can prepare equipment and assist safely during intubation and extubation
2. Learners can respond correctly to high pressure and low volume ventilator alarms
3. Learners can maintain cuff pressure and oral care within bundle requirements
4. Learners can recognise a displaced or blocked airway and call for help immediately

## Who Can Take This Course
ICU, HDU and emergency staff nurses, respiratory therapists, nursing in-charges, operation theatre and recovery nurses, Medical Officers new to critical care, and nursing students on intensive care posting.
