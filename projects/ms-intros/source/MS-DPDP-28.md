## Short Description
Protect patient data and meet Digital Personal Data Protection Act duties for health records.

## Full Description
This course explains the duties of a hospital as a data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, including notice, consent, purpose limitation, retention, security safeguards, breach notification and the rights of patients as data principals. It covers practical controls for electronic medical records, registration desks, laboratory reports, imaging archives and third-party vendors handling health data.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain hospital obligations as a data fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023
2. Apply notice and consent requirements at registration and during clinical care
3. Identify security safeguards for electronic and paper patient records
4. Plan breach detection, escalation and notification steps within the hospital

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can handle a patient request to access or correct personal data
2. Learners can recognise and report a personal data breach without delay
3. Learners can apply access controls and retention rules to health records
4. Learners can review vendor arrangements involving patient data for compliance gaps

## Who Can Take This Course
Hospital administrators, IT and health information staff, medical records officers, registration and billing staff, Medical Officers, nurses, laboratory and radiology staff; required annually for everyone handling patient information.
