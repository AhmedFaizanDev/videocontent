A core bedside skill standardised across your nursing workforce.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme standardises peripheral intravenous cannulation and infusion therapy practice. It covers vein selection, aseptic non-touch technique, cannula sizing, securement, dressing, flushing, infusion device use, drug compatibility basics and recognition of phlebitis, infiltration and extravasation. NABH 6th Edition and infection control programmes monitor peripheral line associated complications, so consistent bedside technique across the nursing workforce directly affects reported infection rates.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain vein anatomy, site selection and cannula sizing for different infusion requirements
2. Demonstrate aseptic non-touch cannulation, securement and transparent dressing application
3. Apply flushing, line maintenance and dressing change schedules to prevent catheter related complications
4. Identify phlebitis, infiltration and extravasation early and initiate the correct management

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can insert a peripheral cannula using aseptic technique on a simulation arm
2. Learners can grade phlebitis using a standard scale and escalate appropriately
3. Learners can set and verify infusion rates on volumetric pumps and syringe pumps
4. Learners can document insertion, site checks and removal in the nursing record

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward and ICU nurses, emergency and day care nurses, dialysis nurses, nursing assistants under supervision, Medical Officers, paramedics, and GNM or BSc nursing students in clinical postings.
