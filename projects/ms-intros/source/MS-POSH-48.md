# POSH — Prevention of Sexual Harassment (MS-POSH-48)

## Course Code

## Course Name
POSH — Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Category
Onboarding, Ethics & HR Compliance

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Statutory awareness under the POSH Act, 2013 — for every employee, every year.

## Full Description
This short statutory awareness course explains the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act 2013, what conduct amounts to sexual harassment, the role and composition of the Internal Committee, how to make a complaint, timelines for enquiry, confidentiality and protection against retaliation. Every hospital, laboratory and health centre covered by the Act must run this awareness programme for its workforce.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the scope and definitions of the POSH Act 2013
2. Identify behaviour that constitutes sexual harassment in a hospital workplace
3. Explain the complaint, enquiry and redressal process under the Act
4. Apply bystander and reporting responsibilities in everyday workplace situations

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can recognise conduct that would amount to sexual harassment at work
2. Learners can describe how to approach the Internal Committee with a complaint
3. Learners can state the confidentiality and anti-retaliation protections available to complainants
4. Learners can respond appropriately when a colleague reports an incident to them

## Who Can Take This Course
Required annually for every employee, including doctors, nurses, technicians, pharmacists, administrative and accounts staff, housekeeping, security, drivers, contract workers, and students or interns placed at the organisation.
