## Short Description
Face assessors with confidence — SOPs, records and mock-audit practice ahead of every assessment cycle.

## Full Description
This twelve-hour programme prepares hospital teams for a NABH 6th Edition assessment. It covers the standards and objective elements, SOP writing, record keeping, quality indicators, committee minutes and corrective action plans, and includes mock-audit practice with assessor-style questioning. Indian hospitals seeking accreditation or renewal need documented evidence across every chapter, and this course shows staff how to build and maintain it.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the structure of NABH 6th Edition chapters, standards and objective elements
2. Apply document control rules to SOPs, policies, manuals and departmental records
3. Plan evidence collection for quality indicators, committee minutes and incident registers
4. Demonstrate mock-audit responses to common assessor questions across clinical departments

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can map departmental practice against the relevant NABH objective elements
2. Learners can draft and revise SOPs that meet document control requirements
3. Learners can assemble an assessment evidence file for their own department
4. Learners can answer assessor questions calmly using records held on site

## Who Can Take This Course
Quality managers, NABH coordinators, department heads, staff nurses, ward in-charges, Medical Officers, laboratory and radiology supervisors, administrative officers and hospital management interns; required for all departments before every assessment cycle.
