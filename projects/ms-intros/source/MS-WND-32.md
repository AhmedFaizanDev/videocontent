## Short Description
Assessment and care tied directly to a monitored quality indicator.

## Full Description
This course covers wound assessment, classification of pressure injuries, risk scoring with recognised scales, repositioning, support surfaces, nutrition, moisture management, dressing selection and documentation of wound progress. Pressure injury rate is a monitored nursing quality indicator under NABH 6th Edition, so wards, intensive care units and long-stay areas need consistent assessment and preventive practice across every shift.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the causes and staging of pressure injuries and chronic wounds
2. Apply a validated risk assessment scale to admitted patients
3. Demonstrate repositioning, skin care and dressing techniques for at-risk patients
4. Use wound documentation and photographs to track healing over time

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can assess and stage a pressure injury accurately at the bedside
2. Learners can plan and deliver a preventive care bundle for high-risk patients
3. Learners can select an appropriate dressing for a given wound type
4. Learners can record pressure injury data for the nursing quality indicator

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, ICU nurses, nursing assistants, physiotherapists, dietitians, Medical Officers, home care nurses and nursing students on clinical posting in medical and surgical wards.
