# Surgical Site Infection (SSI) Prevention (MS-SSI-58)

## Course Code

## Category

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Evidence-based SSI bundles from pre-op to discharge, with surveillance and feedback.

## Full Description
This course sets out the evidence-based surgical site infection prevention bundle across the pre-operative, intra-operative and post-operative phases, including hair removal, skin preparation, timing of prophylaxis, normothermia, theatre discipline, wound dressing and discharge advice. It also covers surveillance, case definitions and feedback to surgical teams. Hospitals report surgical site infection rates as a quality indicator under NABH 6th Edition requirements.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the risk factors and pathogenesis of surgical site infection
2. Apply the pre-operative, intra-operative and post-operative elements of the prevention bundle
3. Demonstrate correct aseptic technique in theatre and during wound dressing
4. Use surveillance definitions to classify and report surgical site infections

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can audit bundle compliance for a list of surgical cases
2. Learners can identify lapses in theatre discipline and correct them promptly
3. Learners can classify a wound infection as superficial, deep or organ space
4. Learners can present surgical site infection rates with feedback to operating teams

## Who Can Take This Course
Surgeons, anaesthetists, operation theatre nurses, scrub and circulating staff, ward nurses, infection control nurses, central sterile supply staff, theatre technicians, and surgical postgraduate residents or interns.
