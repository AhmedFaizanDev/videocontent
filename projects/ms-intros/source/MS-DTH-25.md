# Death Certification & Handling of Deaths (MS-DTH-25)

## Course Code
MS-DTH-25

## Course Name
Death Certification & Handling of Deaths

## Category
Medico-Legal & Regulatory Compliance

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Certify deaths correctly and manage bodies, documentation and difficult conversations.

## Full Description
A three-hour refresher on death certification and handling of deaths in hospitals. It covers the Medical Certification of Cause of Death form, cause of death sequencing, registration timelines under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, brought-dead and medico-legal cases, police intimation, postmortem referral, body identification and release, and communicating a death to the family with care.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the correct completion and sequencing of the medical certificate of cause of death
2. Identify cases requiring police intimation, inquest or postmortem examination
3. Apply hospital procedure for body identification, mortuary handover and release to relatives
4. Demonstrate sensitive communication of a death to family members and attendants

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can complete a cause of death certificate without common sequencing errors
2. Learners can decide when a death becomes a medico-legal case
3. Learners can follow the documentation trail from ward to mortuary to release
4. Learners can break news of a death clearly and respectfully to relatives

## Who Can Take This Course
Medical Officers, resident doctors, consultants, nursing staff, ward in-charges, mortuary attendants, medical records officers, front office and medico-legal clerks, and medical interns; required for all staff certifying or processing deaths.
