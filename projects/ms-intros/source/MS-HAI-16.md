## Short Description
Surveillance and response for infection-control nurses and the HIC committee.

## Full Description
An applied programme for infection control nurses and hospital infection control committee members. It covers surveillance definitions, denominator data, device utilisation and infection rates, data collection forms, trend analysis, feedback to units, outbreak investigation steps, environmental sampling decisions and reporting. NABH 6th Edition expects hospitals to monitor healthcare-associated infection rates and act on the findings through a documented committee process.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply standard surveillance definitions for device-associated and surgical site infections
2. Calculate infection rates using correct numerator and denominator data
3. Interpret surveillance trends and present findings to the infection control committee
4. Plan a structured outbreak investigation including case definition and control measures

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can run daily surveillance rounds and complete data collection forms accurately
2. Learners can compute and chart device utilisation ratios and infection rates monthly
3. Learners can give unit-level feedback that leads to a documented corrective action
4. Learners can lead the initial steps of an outbreak investigation with the committee

## Who Can Take This Course
Infection control nurses and officers, microbiologists, HIC committee members, quality coordinators, ICU and ward in-charges, nursing superintendents, and postgraduate microbiology or public health trainees posted to infection control.
