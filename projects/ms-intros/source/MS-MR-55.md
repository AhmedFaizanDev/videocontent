# Medical Records & Clinical Documentation (MS-MR-55)

## Course Code

## Course Name

## Category
Documentation, Coding & Digital Health

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Complete, legible records — a recurring audit finding, addressed head-on.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers medical record content, legibility, timeliness, entry signing and correction, initial assessment and reassessment notes, discharge summary quality, record retention and release, and record completeness audit. Incomplete documentation is a recurring NABH 6th Edition finding and weakens the hospital's position in medico-legal matters. Patient record confidentiality under the DPDP Act 2023 is also addressed.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the mandatory contents, sequence and retention periods for inpatient and outpatient records
2. Apply rules on legibility, dating, timing, signature, authentication and correction of entries
3. Demonstrate writing an initial assessment, progress note and discharge summary to required standards
4. Plan a medical record completeness audit with sampling, scoring and feedback to departments

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can assemble a complete patient record file in the prescribed order
2. Learners can correct an erroneous entry without obliterating the original information
3. Learners can write a discharge summary containing diagnosis, treatment, medication and follow-up instructions
4. Learners can score a sample of records for completeness and report gaps to clinicians

## Who Can Take This Course
Medical records officers and technicians, staff nurses, resident doctors and Medical Officers, consultants, quality and audit staff, admission and discharge desk personnel, coding staff, and medical or health information management interns.
