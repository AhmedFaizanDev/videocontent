# Spill Management & Chemical Safety (MS-SPILL-18)

## Course Code
MS-SPILL-18

## Course Name
Spill Management & Chemical Safety

## Category
Waste, Fire & Facility Safety

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Safe handling of spills, mercury and hazardous chemicals across the facility.

## Full Description
A short refresher on managing spills and hazardous chemicals across hospital areas. It covers blood and body fluid spill kits, mercury spill handling, chemical and cytotoxic spills, disinfectant selection and contact time, ventilation, personal protective equipment, safety data sheets and post-spill waste disposal under BMW Rules 2016. Every ward, laboratory and housekeeping team needs this capability on every shift.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify spill types and the correct kit and disinfectant for each
2. Demonstrate step-by-step containment and clean-up of a blood spill
3. Explain safe handling of mercury and cytotoxic spills within the facility
4. Use safety data sheets to select protective equipment and storage conditions

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can assemble and check a complete spill management kit
2. Learners can clean a body fluid spill using correct disinfectant concentration and contact time
3. Learners can dispose of spill waste in the correct colour-coded container
4. Learners can locate safety data sheets and act on their handling instructions

## Who Can Take This Course
Housekeeping staff and supervisors, staff nurses, ward attendants, laboratory technicians, CSSD and laundry workers, pharmacy staff, facility and safety officers, and nursing or MLT students; required annually for all staff.
