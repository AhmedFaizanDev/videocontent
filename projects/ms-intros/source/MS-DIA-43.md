## Short Description
Skills for the fast-growing dialysis and nephrology service line.

## Full Description
This twelve-hour programme prepares nurses for haemodialysis practice. It covers renal failure and dialysis principles, vascular access care for fistulae and catheters, machine setup and priming, dialyser reuse policy, anticoagulation, intradialytic monitoring and complications, water treatment and dialysate quality, and infection control including hepatitis segregation. Dialysis units under NABH 6th Edition and PMNDP require documented staff competence for these tasks.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain diffusion, ultrafiltration and dialysis adequacy as applied to routine haemodialysis prescriptions
2. Demonstrate arteriovenous fistula cannulation and aseptic handling of central venous dialysis catheters
3. Apply intradialytic monitoring protocols to detect hypotension, cramps, bleeding and access complications
4. Use water treatment, disinfection and machine reprocessing schedules to maintain dialysate quality

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can set up, prime and commence a dialysis session following unit protocol
2. Learners can manage intradialytic hypotension and other common complications under standing orders
3. Learners can assess and document vascular access patency, infection signs and cannulation technique
4. Learners can maintain dialysis records including dry weight, ultrafiltration volume and machine disinfection logs

## Who Can Take This Course
Dialysis nurses and technicians, staff nurses moving into nephrology services, nephrology Medical Officers, ICU nurses supporting acute dialysis, biomedical technicians handling dialysis machines, and nursing or dialysis technology students.
