## Short Description
Electrical safety, signage and utilities under the NABH facility-management chapter.

## Full Description
This course covers electrical safety, medical gas pipelines, water and power backup, lifts, signage, wayfinding, hazardous material storage, fire safety systems and planned preventive maintenance of the hospital building. The NABH 6th Edition facility management and safety chapter requires documented safety rounds, risk assessment and mock drills. The content also supports Kayakalp and NQAS assessment of infrastructure and environmental upkeep in public facilities.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the facility and environmental safety requirements applicable to hospital buildings
2. Identify electrical, gas, water and structural hazards during safety rounds
3. Apply signage, storage and access control standards across clinical and utility areas
4. Plan preventive maintenance schedules and mock drills for critical utilities

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can conduct a documented facility safety round and record findings
2. Learners can respond correctly to a utility failure or medical gas alarm
3. Learners can verify that hazardous materials are stored and labelled appropriately
4. Learners can maintain maintenance and drill records expected at assessment

## Who Can Take This Course
Facility and maintenance engineers, biomedical staff, electricians, safety officers, housekeeping supervisors, security supervisors, nursing in-charges, administrators and quality coordinators; required annually for all staff with safety duties.
