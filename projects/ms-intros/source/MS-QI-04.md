## Short Description
Collect, monitor and act on quality indicators — the continuous-improvement discipline most hospitals lack.

## Full Description
This six-hour programme covers the selection, definition, collection and analysis of hospital quality indicators, and the conduct of clinical audit cycles. NABH 6th Edition requires monitored indicators across clinical and managerial areas, and NQAS assessment uses similar measures. Participants learn to build indicator dashboards, run root cause analysis and close audit loops with documented corrective action.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the structure, process and outcome indicator framework used in hospital quality monitoring
2. Apply operational definitions, numerators and denominators to construct reliable quality indicators
3. Interpret run charts and control charts to separate common cause from special cause variation
4. Plan a complete clinical audit cycle with criteria, standards, re-audit and corrective action

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can define a hospital quality indicator with a written operational definition and data source
2. Learners can compile monthly indicator data and present it accurately to a quality committee
3. Learners can identify contributing causes of an adverse trend using a structured analysis tool
4. Learners can complete and document one clinical audit cycle including re-audit findings

## Who Can Take This Course
Quality managers and coordinators, NABH nodal officers, nursing superintendents, ward in-charges, Medical Officers, department heads, medical records staff, and hospital administration students or interns supporting quality cells.
