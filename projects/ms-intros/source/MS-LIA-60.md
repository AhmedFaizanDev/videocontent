## Short Description
Plan, conduct and report ISO 15189 internal audits — the trained auditors NABL expects on staff.

## Full Description
This sixteen-hour certification-grade programme trains laboratory staff to plan, conduct, report and follow up internal audits against ISO 15189:2022. NABL requires accredited medical laboratories to maintain a competent internal audit team and a documented audit programme. The course covers audit planning, checklist design, objective evidence gathering, interviewing technique, nonconformity grading, root cause analysis and corrective action verification.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the clause structure and management system requirements of ISO 15189:2022
2. Plan a risk-based annual internal audit programme covering all laboratory processes and sites
3. Demonstrate audit interviewing, sampling and objective evidence collection during a simulated audit
4. Apply nonconformity grading, root cause analysis and corrective action verification to audit findings

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can prepare an audit plan and clause-referenced checklist for an assigned laboratory section
2. Learners can conduct an opening meeting, audit trail and closing meeting with appropriate professional conduct
3. Learners can write clear nonconformity statements citing the requirement, evidence and departure observed
4. Learners can verify closure of corrective actions and record objective evidence of effectiveness

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory quality managers, technical managers, senior laboratory technicians and technologists, consultant pathologists and microbiologists, biochemists, quality officers, and postgraduate pathology residents. Required for staff nominated to the laboratory internal audit team.
