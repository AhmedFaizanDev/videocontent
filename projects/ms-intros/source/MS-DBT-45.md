# Diabetes Care & Management (MS-DBT-45)

## Course Code
MS-DBT-45

## Course Name
Diabetes Care & Management

## Category
Specialty & Emerging Clinical

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Broadly applicable diabetes care for nurses and patient educators.

## Full Description
A three-hour refresher on practical diabetes care for hospital and primary care teams. It covers types of diabetes, diagnostic criteria, glycaemic targets, oral agents and insulin, injection technique and site rotation, glucose monitoring, recognition of hypoglycaemia and hyperglycaemic emergencies, foot care, diet and activity counselling, and follow-up of patients at health and wellness centres.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain diabetes types, diagnostic criteria and glycaemic targets used in clinical practice
2. Demonstrate correct insulin injection technique, storage and site rotation to patients
3. Identify hypoglycaemia and hyperglycaemic emergencies and initiate immediate management
4. Plan patient counselling on diet, foot care, monitoring and follow-up visits

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can teach a newly diagnosed patient to self-administer insulin safely
2. Learners can treat a hypoglycaemic episode promptly and record the event
3. Learners can perform a basic diabetic foot screening during a routine visit
4. Learners can explain monitoring and follow-up schedules in simple local language

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ANMs, ASHAs, Medical Officers at PHCs and health and wellness centres, diabetes educators, dietitians, pharmacists, community health officers and nursing students.
