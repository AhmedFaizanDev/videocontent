## Short Description
Meet PC-PNDT obligations for imaging units — non-compliance carries severe legal penalties.

## Full Description
A focused compliance refresher for imaging and diagnostic units registered under the PC-PNDT Act. The course covers registration and renewal, mandatory display of notices, Form F completion and submission, record retention, prohibition of sex determination and disclosure, machine registration, and inspection readiness. Non-compliance carries suspension of registration and criminal penalties, so every genetic clinic and ultrasound centre must train its staff.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the prohibitions and duties imposed by the PC-PNDT Act
2. Apply correct Form F completion, signing and monthly submission practice
3. Identify mandatory displays, registration documents and record retention requirements
4. Plan inspection readiness for the district appropriate authority visit

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can complete Form F accurately for every eligible ultrasound examination
2. Learners can maintain registration, machine and staff records in inspection-ready order
3. Learners can explain prohibited communication about foetal sex to patients and relatives
4. Learners can identify common documentation lapses that attract penalties during inspection

## Who Can Take This Course
Radiologists, sonologists, obstetricians, radiographers and ultrasound technicians, imaging receptionists and record clerks, centre owners, hospital administrators and compliance officers; required for all staff of registered imaging units.
