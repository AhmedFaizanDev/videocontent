## Full Description
A course on safe transfusion practice for blood centre and clinical teams. It covers consent and indication, sample collection for grouping and cross-match, bedside identity checks, storage and cold chain, transport, administration and monitoring, recognition and management of transfusion reactions, haemovigilance reporting and traceability. NABH 6th Edition and blood centre licensing requirements both expect documented competence in these steps.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the transfusion chain from request and cross-match to bedside administration
2. Apply bedside identity and compatibility checks before starting any transfusion
3. Identify acute and delayed transfusion reactions and their immediate management
4. Use the haemovigilance reporting pathway for adverse transfusion events

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can perform the two-person bedside check and document it correctly
2. Learners can maintain cold chain and return unused units within permitted time
3. Learners can stop a transfusion and manage a suspected reaction promptly
4. Learners can complete a transfusion reaction investigation form and traceability record

## Who Can Take This Course
Blood centre medical officers and technicians, transfusion medicine staff, staff nurses, ICU and operation theatre nurses, resident doctors, ward in-charges, and MLT or nursing students posted to transfusion services.
