## Short Description
Four-colour segregation, barcoding and annual reporting — legally required every year.

## Full Description
A six-hour course on the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 and their amendments. It covers the four-colour segregation scheme, pre-treatment of laboratory waste, sharps handling, barcoding and bag labelling, storage limits, transport to the common treatment facility, spill management, accident reporting and the annual report to the State Pollution Control Board. Training is a legal duty for every healthcare establishment in India.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the four-colour segregation categories prescribed under BMW Rules 2016
2. Apply correct labelling, barcoding and weighing practice at every waste generation point
3. Demonstrate safe sharps handling, spill response and needle-stick injury reporting
4. Plan record keeping, storage timelines and the annual return to the pollution control board

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can segregate any item of hospital waste into the correct container
2. Learners can complete daily waste registers and barcode records without omissions
3. Learners can manage a blood or chemical spill using the prescribed method
4. Learners can compile the data needed for the establishment's annual BMW return

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, housekeeping staff and supervisors, laboratory technicians, ward boys, OT and dialysis staff, infection control nurses, waste handlers, Medical Officers and interns; required annually for all clinical and non-clinical staff.
