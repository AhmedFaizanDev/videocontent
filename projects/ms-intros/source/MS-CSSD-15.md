## Short Description
Decontamination and sterile-supply standards for CSSD, OT and housekeeping.

## Full Description
This six-hour course covers central sterile supply practice: unidirectional workflow, receiving and decontamination, cleaning agents and ultrasonic washers, inspection and packing, autoclave loading and cycle selection, chemical and biological indicators, Bowie-Dick testing, sterile storage, shelf life and recall. NABH 6th Edition and infection control programmes in Indian hospitals depend on a CSSD that can prove every load was processed and monitored correctly.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain unidirectional workflow from decontamination through packing, sterilisation and sterile storage
2. Apply correct cleaning, disinfection and inspection methods to surgical instruments and sets
3. Use chemical, biological and Bowie-Dick indicators to monitor each sterilisation cycle
4. Identify conditions that compromise sterility during storage, transport and issue

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can process an instrument set through every CSSD stage correctly
2. Learners can read indicator results and withhold a failed load from issue
3. Learners can maintain load records that allow a recall to be traced
4. Learners can judge when a stored pack is no longer fit for use

## Who Can Take This Course
CSSD technicians and supervisors, OT nurses, ward nurses handling sterile supplies, infection control nurses, housekeeping supervisors, biomedical staff and nursing or OT technician interns posted to sterile services.
