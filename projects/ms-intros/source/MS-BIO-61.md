# Laboratory Biosafety & Biosecurity (MS-BIO-61)

## Course Code
MS-BIO-61

## Course Name
Laboratory Biosafety & Biosecurity

## Category
Laboratory Quality & NABL (ISO 15189)

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Biosafety levels, PPE, safe specimen handling, spills and exposure management in the lab.

## Full Description
This course covers biosafety levels, risk assessment, personal protective equipment, safe specimen collection and transport, centrifuge and aerosol precautions, disinfection, spill management and post-exposure prophylaxis. Laboratories working towards or holding NABL accreditation under ISO 15189:2022 must demonstrate documented biosafety practice, and disposal of laboratory waste must follow the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 in every hospital and standalone centre.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain biosafety levels and the risk group classification of common pathogens
2. Apply correct personal protective equipment selection, donning and doffing in laboratory areas
3. Demonstrate safe handling, packaging and transport of clinical specimens
4. Use approved procedures to manage spills, exposures and laboratory waste

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can carry out a biosafety risk assessment for their own workbench
2. Learners can respond correctly to a spill or needlestick exposure
3. Learners can segregate laboratory waste as required under BMW Rules 2016
4. Learners can maintain biosafety records expected during a NABL assessment

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory technicians, technologists, phlebotomists, pathologists, microbiologists, laboratory quality managers, housekeeping staff working in laboratory areas and DMLT or MLT students on placement; required annually for all laboratory personnel.
