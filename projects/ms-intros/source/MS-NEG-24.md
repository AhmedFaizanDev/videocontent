# Medical Negligence & Liability Prevention (MS-NEG-24)

## Category

## Course Type

## Learning Hours
12

## Short Description
Understand duty of care and negligence, and the everyday practices that prevent claims.

## Full Description
This certification-grade programme explains duty of care, standard of care, breach, causation and damages, consumer forum and criminal proceedings, consent, medical records as evidence, and the everyday documentation habits that prevent claims. Indian hospitals functioning under the Clinical Establishments Act and accredited under NABH 6th Edition need clinicians and administrators who understand liability and can defend care through complete, contemporaneous records.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the legal elements of medical negligence as applied in India
2. Apply valid consent processes for procedures, anaesthesia and high-risk interventions
3. Identify documentation practices that reduce exposure to negligence claims
4. Plan an institutional response to a complaint, notice or legal proceeding

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can take and record consent that meets legal and accreditation expectations
2. Learners can write case notes that stand as reliable evidence later
3. Learners can recognise situations carrying high medico-legal risk and escalate early
4. Learners can handle medical record requests and summons through correct institutional channels

## Who Can Take This Course
Consultants, Medical Officers, postgraduate residents, dentists, nursing superintendents, ward in-charges, medical records officers, hospital administrators and legal cell staff; required for all clinicians providing direct patient care.
