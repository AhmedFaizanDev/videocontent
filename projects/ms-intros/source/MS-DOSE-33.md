## Short Description
Reduce the most common nursing error with confident calculation.

## Full Description
This six-hour course strengthens safe medication administration and dosage calculation. It covers the rights of administration, prescription reading, unit conversion, tablet and liquid dose calculation, paediatric weight-based dosing, infusion and drip rate calculation, insulin and heparin dosing, high-alert medication controls, look-alike sound-alike drugs and error reporting. Medication error remains a common cause of patient harm in Indian hospital wards.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply the rights of medication administration at every drug round
2. Use unit conversion and formulae to calculate tablet, liquid and paediatric doses
3. Interpret infusion orders and calculate drip rates and pump settings accurately
4. Identify high-alert and look-alike sound-alike medications requiring independent double checking

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can calculate a weight-based paediatric dose without arithmetic error
2. Learners can set an infusion rate correctly for a prescribed volume and duration
3. Learners can carry out an independent double check on a high-alert medication
4. Learners can report a medication error or near miss through hospital channels

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, ICU and paediatric nurses, ANMs, GNMs, pharmacists and pharmacy technicians, Medical Officers and nursing students or interns handling medication administration.
