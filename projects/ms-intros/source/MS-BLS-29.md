# Basic & Advanced Cardiac Life Support (BLS/ACLS) (MS-BLS-29)

## Course Code
MS-BLS-29

## Course Name
Basic & Advanced Cardiac Life Support (BLS/ACLS)

## Category
Nursing Skills & Upskilling

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
12

## Short Description
High-quality CPR, airway basics and rapid response expected of clinical staff.

## Full Description
A twelve-hour certification-grade course in basic and advanced cardiac life support following current resuscitation guidance. It covers recognition of cardiac arrest, high-quality chest compressions, bag-mask ventilation, AED and defibrillator use, airway adjuncts, rhythm recognition, resuscitation drugs, team roles and post-resuscitation care, with hands-on manikin practice. Indian hospitals need trained responders on every shift and NABH expects documented life support training.

## Course Objectives
1. Demonstrate high-quality chest compressions and ventilation on adult and paediatric manikins
2. Apply the basic life support sequence including AED use and team switching
3. Interpret shockable and non-shockable rhythms and select the correct ACLS intervention
4. Plan resuscitation team roles, drug administration and post-resuscitation transfer of care

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can start effective compressions within seconds of recognising cardiac arrest
2. Learners can operate an AED or defibrillator safely during a resuscitation
3. Learners can lead or support a code team using assigned roles
4. Learners can hand over a resuscitated patient with a complete clinical summary

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ICU and emergency nurses, Medical Officers, resident doctors, consultants, paramedics, technicians, physiotherapists and nursing or medical interns; required for all clinical staff with periodic recertification.
