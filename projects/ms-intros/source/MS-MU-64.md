## Short Description
Verify methods before use and estimate measurement uncertainty as NABL-112 requires.

## Full Description
This twelve-hour programme covers verification of manufacturer claims, validation of laboratory developed or modified methods, and estimation of measurement uncertainty. ISO 15189:2022 and NABL guidance require laboratories to verify precision, trueness, linearity and reportable range before clinical use, and to state uncertainty for quantitative results. Participants work through worked calculations using their own instrument and quality control data.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain when verification is sufficient and when full method validation becomes necessary
2. Apply protocols for precision, trueness, linearity, detection limit and reportable range studies
3. Interpret comparison study data against a reference method using regression and bias plots
4. Use internal quality control and calibrator data to estimate measurement uncertainty for quantitative tests

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can design and document a verification protocol with defined acceptance criteria before test introduction
2. Learners can calculate within-run and between-run precision from replicate data and judge acceptability
3. Learners can produce a measurement uncertainty budget for a routine quantitative analyte
4. Learners can compile a verification report suitable for review during a NABL assessment

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory technical managers, quality managers, senior technologists in biochemistry, haematology and immunoassay, consultant pathologists and biochemists, method validation officers, and postgraduate laboratory medicine trainees.
