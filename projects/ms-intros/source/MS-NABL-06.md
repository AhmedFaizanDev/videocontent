## Short Description
Quality-system essentials for diagnostic and pathology teams pursuing NABL.

## Full Description
A twelve-hour certification-grade course on the quality management system required by NABL under ISO 15189:2022. It covers the quality manual, competence assessment, equipment calibration, method verification, uncertainty of measurement, sample handling, nonconformity management and internal audit. Hospital laboratories applying for NABL accreditation or maintaining it need staff who understand these clauses and can produce the records assessors ask for.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the management and technical requirements of ISO 15189:2022 for laboratories
2. Apply competence assessment and training records to laboratory personnel management
3. Plan equipment calibration, method verification and measurement uncertainty activities for the laboratory
4. Use internal audit and nonconformity procedures to close quality system gaps

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can locate the ISO 15189:2022 clause behind any laboratory quality requirement
2. Learners can prepare a quality manual section for their own laboratory
3. Learners can conduct an internal audit and record findings with corrective actions
4. Learners can maintain calibration and verification records that satisfy NABL assessors

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory quality managers, technical heads, pathologists, microbiologists, biochemists, senior lab technicians, phlebotomists, laboratory documentation staff and DMLT or MLT interns; required for teams pursuing or renewing NABL accreditation.
