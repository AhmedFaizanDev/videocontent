## Short Description
Resolve complaints well — for front office, PROs and duty managers.

## Full Description
A short skills module on handling patient and attendant complaints. It covers receiving a grievance calmly, active listening, acknowledging concerns, de-escalating anger, recording the complaint, timelines for response, escalation levels, closing the loop with the complainant and using complaint trends for improvement. NABH 6th Edition requires hospitals to have a documented grievance redressal process with defined response times.

## Course Objectives
1. Apply active listening and de-escalation techniques when receiving an angry complaint
2. Explain the hospital grievance process, escalation levels and response timelines
3. Demonstrate accurate recording and acknowledgement of a patient complaint
4. Interpret complaint trends to recommend service improvements to management

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can calm an agitated attendant and gather the facts clearly
2. Learners can log a complaint with complete details and route it appropriately
3. Learners can close a grievance with the complainant within the stated timeline
4. Learners can summarise monthly complaint themes for the patient experience committee

## Who Can Take This Course
Front office and reception staff, patient relations officers, duty managers, billing and admission staff, ward in-charges, nursing supervisors, security supervisors, quality coordinators, and hospital administration students on internship.
