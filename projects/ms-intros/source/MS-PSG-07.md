## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
IPSG in practice — correct identification, safe surgery and effective handovers.

## Full Description
This six-hour refresher covers the International Patient Safety Goals as applied in Indian hospitals: correct patient identification, effective communication and handover, safe use of high-alert medications, correct site and correct procedure surgery, infection prevention and reduction of fall risk. The NABH 6th Edition patient safety requirements rest on these goals, and every patient-facing team needs shared practice around them.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain each International Patient Safety Goal and its relevance to hospital care
2. Apply two-identifier patient identification at every point of care and procedure
3. Demonstrate structured handover and read-back for verbal and telephone orders
4. Identify fall risk, high-alert medication risk and surgical site verification failures

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can identify patients correctly before medication, sampling and procedures
2. Learners can hand over a patient using a structured communication format
3. Learners can flag high-alert medications and apply the required storage controls
4. Learners can complete a fall risk assessment and set preventive measures

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, Medical Officers, resident doctors, technicians, pharmacists, physiotherapists, front office and housekeeping staff, and nursing or medical interns; required annually for all patient-facing staff.
