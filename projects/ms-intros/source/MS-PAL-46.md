# Palliative & End-of-Life Care (MS-PAL-46)

## Course Code
MS-PAL-46

## Course Name
Palliative & End-of-Life Care

## Category
Specialty & Emerging Clinical

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
6

## Short Description
Compassionate, skilled care for a growing and under-served need.

## Full Description
Palliative care needs in India far exceed available services. This course covers identifying patients who benefit from palliative care, pain assessment and the WHO analgesic approach, opioid access and safe prescribing, symptom control, wound and mouth care, communication of serious news, family and caregiver support, advance care planning, and care during the final days including bereavement support.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify patients and families who would benefit from a palliative care approach
2. Apply structured pain and symptom assessment tools at the bedside
3. Explain WHO analgesic principles and safe use of oral morphine
4. Demonstrate compassionate communication when discussing prognosis and goals of care

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can assess and document pain using a validated scoring tool
2. Learners can manage common symptoms such as breathlessness, nausea and constipation
3. Learners can hold a difficult conversation with a patient's family respectfully
4. Learners can recognise the dying phase and adjust care and monitoring accordingly

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, Medical Officers, oncology and medicine unit doctors, home care nurses, physiotherapists, medical social workers, counsellors, hospice volunteers, and nursing or medical students on palliative care posting.
