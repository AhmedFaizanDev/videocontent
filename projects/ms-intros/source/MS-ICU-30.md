# Critical Care / ICU Nursing (MS-ICU-30)

## Course Code
MS-ICU-30

## Course Name
Critical Care / ICU Nursing

## Category
Nursing Skills & Upskilling

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
16

## Short Description
Monitoring, ventilated patients and escalation in high-dependency settings.

## Full Description
A certification-grade nursing programme for high-dependency and intensive care settings. It covers haemodynamic monitoring, arterial blood gas interpretation, vasoactive drug administration, sedation and pain assessment, nutrition, prevention of device-associated infection, pressure injury prevention, early warning scores and escalation. Indian hospitals expanding critical care capacity need nurses who can recognise deterioration early and act within defined protocols.

## Course Objectives
1. Interpret haemodynamic parameters, early warning scores and arterial blood gas results
2. Apply safe administration and titration practice for vasoactive and sedative infusions
3. Demonstrate systematic assessment and handover of a critically ill patient
4. Plan nursing care that prevents pressure injury, delirium and device-associated infection

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can recognise clinical deterioration and escalate using the hospital protocol
2. Learners can interpret a basic arterial blood gas and report significant abnormalities
3. Learners can manage infusion pumps and titrate within prescribed limits safely
4. Learners can deliver a structured ICU handover covering systems, lines and pending tasks

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses posted to ICU, HDU, emergency and post-operative units, ICU in-charges, nursing supervisors, nurses preparing for critical care roles, and final-year nursing students or interns on critical care posting.
