## Short Description
Run and interpret IQC with Levey-Jennings charts, and participate in external quality assessment.

## Full Description
This twelve-hour course teaches daily internal quality control for hospital laboratories. It covers control material selection, mean and standard deviation setting, Levey-Jennings charts, Westgard multirules, error classification, root cause investigation and participation in external quality assessment or proficiency testing. ISO 15189:2022 and NABL require documented IQC and EQAS performance, so technologists must run, interpret and act on control data every shift.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the purpose of internal quality control in clinical laboratory testing
2. Use control material to establish laboratory mean, standard deviation and coefficient of variation
3. Interpret Levey-Jennings charts and apply Westgard multirules to accept or reject runs
4. Plan corrective action and EQAS participation following control failures or poor scores

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can plot and read a Levey-Jennings chart for any analyte
2. Learners can distinguish random error from systematic shift or trend in control data
3. Learners can decide when a run must be rejected and repeated
4. Learners can document EQAS results and investigate unsatisfactory proficiency testing scores

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory technologists, senior lab technicians, section in-charges, quality managers, pathologists, biochemists, microbiologists and DMLT or BSc MLT interns; suited to teams maintaining NABL accredited testing.
