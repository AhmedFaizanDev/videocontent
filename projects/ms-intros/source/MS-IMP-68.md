## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Meet ISO 15189 impartiality and confidentiality requirements for every lab employee.

## Full Description
This three-hour refresher explains the impartiality, confidentiality and ethical conduct duties that ISO 15189:2022 places on every laboratory employee. It covers conflict of interest declaration, resistance to commercial or clinical pressure on results, patient information handling, and consent for use of samples. Indian laboratories also handle patient data under the DPDP Act 2023, which the course addresses in practical terms.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain impartiality and confidentiality requirements applicable to all laboratory personnel under ISO 15189:2022
2. Identify situations that create a conflict of interest or undue pressure on reporting
3. Apply laboratory rules for handling, sharing and releasing patient identifiable information
4. Demonstrate correct escalation when confidentiality is breached or impartiality is threatened

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can declare a conflict of interest using the laboratory's prescribed format and timing
2. Learners can decide correctly whether a report may be released to a given requester
3. Learners can describe safeguards protecting personal data held in laboratory records and systems
4. Learners can report a suspected confidentiality breach through the correct internal channel

## Who Can Take This Course
All laboratory employees including technicians, technologists, phlebotomists, reception and billing staff, data entry operators, housekeeping assigned to the lab, consultants and managers. Required annually for every person working in the laboratory, including interns and trainees.
