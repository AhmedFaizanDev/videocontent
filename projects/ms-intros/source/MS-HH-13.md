# Hand Hygiene & WHO 5 Moments (MS-HH-13)

## Course Code
MS-HH-13

## Course Name
Hand Hygiene & WHO 5 Moments

## Category
Infection Prevention & Control

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
A short refresher on the practice audited continuously across every ward.

## Full Description
This three-hour refresher covers the WHO Five Moments for Hand Hygiene, handrub and handwash technique, glove use and the common reasons compliance fails at the bedside. Hand hygiene compliance is audited continuously and is a reported infection control indicator under NABH 6th Edition. The course suits wards, ICUs, dialysis units, laboratories and outpatient areas in hospitals and primary health centres.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain how hands transmit healthcare associated infection between patients and the care environment
2. Identify the five moments for hand hygiene during routine bedside and procedural care
3. Demonstrate correct handrub and handwash technique with adequate contact time and coverage
4. Apply appropriate glove use without substituting gloves for hand hygiene

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can name each of the five moments and give a bedside example of each
2. Learners can perform handrub covering all surfaces within the recommended duration
3. Learners can choose correctly between alcohol handrub and soap and water
4. Learners can complete a simple hand hygiene compliance observation on their own unit

## Who Can Take This Course
All clinical and support staff including nurses, doctors, technicians, physiotherapists, housekeeping, dietary and transport staff, plus nursing, medical and paramedical students. Required annually for every clinical and non-clinical staff member.
