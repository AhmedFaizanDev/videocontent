## Short Description
Prevent the errors most scrutinised in audits — LASA drugs and high-alert protocols.

## Full Description
Medication errors remain among the most scrutinised findings in hospital audits. This course covers safe prescribing, look-alike sound-alike drug separation, high-alert medication protocols, concentrated electrolyte control, independent double checks, labelling of prepared doses, storage, and error reporting. NABH 6th Edition medication management standards expect hospitals to define and train these controls across clinical and pharmacy teams.

## Course Objectives
1. Identify look-alike sound-alike and high-alert medicines used in the hospital
2. Apply independent double-check procedures before administering high-risk medications
3. Explain safe storage, labelling and control of concentrated electrolytes
4. Use the incident reporting system for medication errors and near misses

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can separate and label LASA medicines correctly in ward stock
2. Learners can perform the five rights check before every medication administration
3. Learners can report a medication error or near miss without delay
4. Learners can explain the hospital protocol for high-alert drug preparation and verification

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, nursing superintendents, pharmacists and pharmacy technicians, Medical Officers, resident doctors, ICU and emergency staff, and nursing or pharmacy students; required annually for all clinical staff.
