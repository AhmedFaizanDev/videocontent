# Point-of-Care Testing (POCT) Quality (MS-POCT-69)

## Course Code
MS-POCT-69

## Course Name
Point-of-Care Testing (POCT) Quality

## Category
Laboratory Quality & NABL (ISO 15189)

## Course Type
Upskilling

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
Quality oversight for glucometers, rapid tests and bedside analysers across wards and PHCs.

## Full Description
This short refresher covers quality oversight of glucometers, rapid diagnostic kits, blood gas analysers and other bedside devices used outside the main laboratory. It addresses operator training, internal quality control, lot verification, reagent storage, result recording and linkage to the parent laboratory. Wards, intensive care units, primary health centres and health and wellness centres need this oversight to keep bedside results dependable and auditable.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the quality risks specific to point-of-care testing outside the laboratory
2. Apply daily internal quality control procedures on bedside analysers and kits
3. Identify storage, lot and expiry problems that affect rapid test results
4. Demonstrate correct recording and reporting of point-of-care test results

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can run and interpret quality control on a bedside device
2. Learners can recognise when a point-of-care result needs laboratory confirmation
3. Learners can maintain device logs, reagent records and operator training evidence
4. Learners can escalate device faults and control failures to the parent laboratory

## Who Can Take This Course
Staff nurses, ward in-charges, ICU and emergency nurses, laboratory technicians, ANMs, Medical Officers and community health officers at primary health centres, plus nursing and MLT students on clinical posting.
