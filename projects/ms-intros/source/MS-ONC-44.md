# Oncology / Chemotherapy Administration (MS-ONC-44)

## Course Code

## Course Name

## Category
Specialty & Emerging Clinical

## Course Type

## Learning Hours
12

## Short Description
Safe handling and administration for oncology day-care and wards.

## Full Description
This certification-grade course covers cytotoxic drug handling, closed system transfer devices, safe reconstitution in a biological safety cabinet, verification and double-check protocols, vesicant extravasation management, infusion reactions, spill kits, patient education and cytotoxic waste disposal under BMW Rules 2016. Oncology day-care units and wards need trained staff because errors with these medicines carry serious and often irreversible harm.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the pharmacology and hazard profile of common cytotoxic medicines
2. Apply safe preparation, transport and administration procedures for chemotherapy
3. Demonstrate management of extravasation, hypersensitivity reactions and cytotoxic spills
4. Use double-check and documentation protocols before every chemotherapy administration

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can verify a chemotherapy prescription against protocol, dose and patient parameters
2. Learners can administer chemotherapy through the correct route with appropriate precautions
3. Learners can manage an extravasation event and document it fully
4. Learners can dispose of cytotoxic waste as required under BMW Rules 2016

## Who Can Take This Course
Oncology nurses, day-care unit nurses, ward nurses, oncologists, Medical Officers, pharmacists preparing cytotoxic doses, pharmacy technicians and nursing students posted in oncology units.
