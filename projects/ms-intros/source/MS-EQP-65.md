## Short Description
Calibration schedules, metrological traceability, maintenance logs and equipment records.

## Full Description
This course explains calibration planning, metrological traceability to reference materials, verification, preventive maintenance schedules, breakdown records and equipment history files. ISO 15189:2022 and NABL assessment expect every measuring instrument in a medical laboratory to have documented calibration status and an identified person responsible. The course helps laboratories build a practical equipment register and avoid reporting results from uncalibrated or overdue analysers.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain metrological traceability and its role in reliable laboratory results
2. Plan calibration and preventive maintenance schedules for laboratory equipment
3. Interpret calibration certificates, measurement uncertainty statements and verification data
4. Use equipment records to demonstrate compliance during a NABL assessment

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can build and maintain an equipment register with calibration due dates
2. Learners can decide whether an instrument is fit for reporting patient results
3. Learners can record breakdowns, repairs and post repair verification correctly
4. Learners can review a calibration certificate and flag traceability shortcomings

## Who Can Take This Course
Laboratory technicians, technologists, biomedical engineers, laboratory quality managers, pathologists, blood bank staff, equipment store in-charges and MLT students; required for all staff operating measuring or diagnostic equipment.
