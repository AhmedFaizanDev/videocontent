# Staff Induction & Onboarding Program (MS-ONB-47)

## Course Code
MS-ONB-47

## Course Name
Staff Induction & Onboarding Program

## Category
Onboarding, Ethics & HR Compliance

## Course Type
Mandatory

## Learning Hours
3

## Short Description
A structured orientation for every new joiner — policies, safety and rights from day one.

## Full Description
This three-hour induction gives every new joiner the essentials before they start work: hospital structure and reporting lines, patient rights and responsibilities, confidentiality and the DPDP Act 2023, hand hygiene and infection control basics, biomedical waste segregation under BMW Rules 2016, fire response, incident reporting and POSH Act 2013 provisions. NABH 6th Edition requires documented orientation for all new staff.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the hospital's structure, reporting lines, policies and code of conduct for new employees
2. Identify patient rights, responsibilities and confidentiality duties applicable to every staff category
3. Apply basic hand hygiene, waste segregation, fire response and incident reporting procedures
4. Explain POSH Act 2013 protections, the Internal Committee and how to raise a complaint

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can describe their reporting line, job responsibilities and applicable hospital policies
2. Learners can segregate common waste items into the correct colour coded container
3. Learners can report an incident, hazard or near miss through the prescribed channel
4. Learners can state where to find policies, the Internal Committee and emergency contact numbers

## Who Can Take This Course
All new joiners across clinical and non-clinical departments including nurses, doctors, technicians, administration, housekeeping, security, dietary and maintenance staff, plus interns, trainees and contract personnel. Required for every new employee before independent duty.

## Delivery Mode
Online, self-paced (mobile and desktop)
