## Short Description
Safer surgery for OT, anaesthesia and surgical nursing teams.

## Full Description
A six-hour course on the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist and wider operation theatre safety. It covers sign in, time out and sign out, site marking, anaesthesia equipment checks, count procedures for sponges and instruments, specimen labelling, theatre discipline and team briefing. NABH 6th Edition expects documented checklist use for every procedure, and this course builds the teamwork that makes it meaningful.

## Course Objectives
1. Explain the three phases of the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist in theatre
2. Demonstrate site marking, patient verification and anaesthesia readiness checks before induction
3. Apply sponge, instrument and sharps counting procedures during and after surgery
4. Plan team briefing and debriefing that surfaces concerns before an incident occurs

## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Learners can run a complete checklist cycle for a scheduled procedure
2. Learners can verify consent, site and implant availability before anaesthesia begins
3. Learners can conduct and document counts and resolve a count discrepancy
4. Learners can label and hand over surgical specimens without identification errors

## Who Can Take This Course
OT nurses, scrub and circulating staff, surgeons, anaesthetists, anaesthesia technicians, OT technicians, OT in-charges, CSSD liaison staff and surgical nursing interns posted to operation theatres.
